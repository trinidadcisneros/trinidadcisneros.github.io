Professional Playbooks

Background Info

Professional Playbooks are structured field guides for analysts, data scientists, and consultants working in professional settings. Unlike technical tutorials that teach you a tool or language, these guides teach you how to do the work — from scoping a request to delivering a polished result.

Why is this important?
Technical skills get you in the door, but professional execution is what builds trust, earns influence, and advances your career. Knowing how to write a SQL query is different from knowing how to scope a dashboard project, validate results, present findings to executives, and document your work for the next person.

What you'll find here:

  • Step-by-step lifecycle guides for common analyst deliverables (dashboards, ad-hoc analyses, metric reviews, A/B tests)
  • Decision trees for navigating ambiguous requests and choosing the right approach
  • Pattern libraries for recurring components (chart types, layout structures, communication formats)
  • Communication templates and talking points for stakeholder interactions
  • Anti-pattern guides covering the political, technical, and process mistakes that derail projects
